SERBIA BASE

Strategic Launch Territory for ZÆTHR Global Expansion

WHY SERBIA

Serbia offers a unique strategic position between Eastern Europe, the Balkans, and the EU. It provides low operational costs, strong logistics infrastructure, and favorable business regulations. ZÆTHR is launching its first operational node in Serbia as the foundation for global expansion.

LEGAL & OPERATIONAL STRUCTURE

The company is being established as a Serbian registered entity with decentralized contractor operations. Initial operations will focus on courier services, logistics testing, and AI-based route optimization development.

EU ACCESS STRATEGY

Serbia acts as a gateway to the European Union market. ZÆTHR will use Serbia as a testing ground before expanding into EU pilot cities with regulatory-compliant models.

LONG-TERM ROLE

Serbia will remain the primary Balkan hub for ZÆTHR operations, engineering talent recruitment, and decentralized logistics infrastructure development.
