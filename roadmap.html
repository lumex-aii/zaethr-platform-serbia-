ZÆTHR ROADMAP

Strategic Expansion Plan for Global AI Logistics Infrastructure

PHASE 0 — FOUNDATION

2026 Q1

Legal company formation in Serbia. Founder-operated courier missions. Initial brand, platform architecture, and decentralized logistics concept validation.

PHASE 1 — LOCAL NETWORK

2026 Q2–Q3

Recruit independent couriers, deploy digital identity system, launch AI-based route optimization prototype and payment automation.

PHASE 2 — REGIONAL EXPANSION

2027

Expansion into Balkan region and EU pilot cities. Partnerships with delivery platforms, smart contracts for logistics tasks.

PHASE 3 — AI LOGISTICS PLATFORM

2028

Full AI orchestration of human couriers, predictive demand modeling, decentralized economic system for global delivery tasks.

PHASE 4 — AUTONOMOUS DELIVERY

2029+

Integration with drones, robotic couriers, and smart city infrastructure. ZÆTHR becomes a global human-machine mobility layer.
