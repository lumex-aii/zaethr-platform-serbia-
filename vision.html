Global Mission

ZÆTHR Platform is designed as a next-generation digital infrastructure combining AI automation, global delivery systems, and decentralized business tools. Our mission is to build a scalable system that empowers individuals and companies to operate globally without borders.

Serbia Launch Strategy

Serbia is selected as the initial launch region due to its strategic position, flexible business environment, and access to the European market. The platform will begin with delivery services and expand into AI-powered operational systems for logistics, finance, and workforce management.

Platform Roadmap

Phase 1 — Local Delivery Network

Motor courier system, Wolt & local partners integration, basic automation.

Phase 2 — AI Business System

AI order routing, smart dispatch, predictive delivery optimization.

Phase 3 — Global Expansion

European cities rollout, multi-language, multi-currency business model.

Phase 4 — Decentralized Platform

Tokenized ecosystem, global freelancer & delivery marketplace.

Founder Vision

Founded by Murat EFE, ZÆTHR Platform represents a long-term vision to merge real-world logistics with AI-driven digital infrastructure. The goal is to create a self-sustaining global system that scales from individual couriers to global enterprises.
