ZÆTHR COURIER NETWORK

Human Mobility Layer for the Global AI Economy

WHY COURIER NETWORK?

ZÆTHR Courier Network is the first step toward a decentralized global delivery infrastructure. Human couriers will be integrated into AI-driven logistics systems, creating a hybrid human-machine economy layer.

CORE SYSTEM MODULES

GLOBAL COURIER HUB

Decentralized network of independent couriers operating under AI orchestration.

AI ROUTING ENGINE

Machine intelligence assigns delivery routes, pricing, and risk scoring.

DIGITAL IDENTITY

Couriers receive verified digital profiles connected to blockchain credentials.

MICRO-ECONOMY SYSTEM

Automated payment, crypto-based rewards, and smart contracts for delivery tasks.

FOUNDER OPERATIONS PHASE

Initial operations will start with founder-led courier missions in Serbia. This phase validates system design, operational models, and economic feasibility before global scale.

FUTURE EXPANSION

After proof of concept, the Courier Network will expand into Europe, Middle East, and global smart cities. Integration with autonomous drones and robotic couriers is planned in later phases.
